# Code of Conduct

The BabelQueue community adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its
Code of Conduct. The full text is available here:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## In short

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity. We expect all participants to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Harassment, personal attacks, and other conduct a reasonable person would consider
inappropriate in a professional setting are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions, and may warn or ban any participant for behavior
they consider inappropriate, in line with the Contributor Covenant.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests,
and discussions — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns confidentially to the project maintainer at
**info@muhammetsafak.com.tr**. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
guidelines linked above.
